Our Story

Professional catering, made fresh for every occasion.

NJ Catering is a professional catering service run by Mr. K. Sathish, serving weddings, corporate events and family functions across Tamil Nadu. In just a few years, we've built a reputation for punctual service, hygienic preparation and menus that stay true to authentic South Indian flavours — from traditional banana leaf meals to elaborate multi-cuisine spreads for large weddings.

Every order is cooked fresh on the day of the event, close to the venue wherever possible, so nothing reaches your guests reheated.

Good to Know

Frequently asked questions

How far in advance should I book?+
For weddings, we recommend booking 3–4 weeks ahead. For smaller home functions, 3–5 days is usually enough depending on guest count.
Do you cook on-site or bring pre-cooked food?+
Wherever the venue allows, our team sets up and cooks fresh on-site — including live counters for dosa, fried rice and sweets.
What's the minimum guest count?+
We typically take on bookings starting from 100 guests onward. For smaller or larger requirements, message us on WhatsApp and we'll work out the best option for your event.
Do you provide staff for serving?+
Yes, serving staff, buffet setup and cleanup can be included — just mention it when you enquire.
